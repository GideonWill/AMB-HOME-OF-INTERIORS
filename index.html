A few helpful things.

What kinds of projects do you take on?+

We design and deliver residential interiors, including kitchens, living and dining spaces, and full-home projects, as well as selected office and commercial interiors.

Where does AMB work?+

Our studio is based in Accra, and we work with clients across Accra and surrounding communities. Share your project location and we can discuss the best way to work together.

What happens after I send my enquiry?+

We’ll review your project details and contact you to arrange an initial conversation about your space, priorities and next steps.
